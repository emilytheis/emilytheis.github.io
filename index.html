Emily Theis

I'm a curious, creative product leader who builds teams and tools for the public good.

I care deeply about making complex systems work better for the people who depend on them. My background in journalism graphics gives me a strong foundation in visual storytelling and quickly navigating complexity. I enjoy building friendly and effective teams that ship quality work backed by clear decisions. Outside of work, I'm a musician, sewist, and runner.

Work

I lead product for CiviForm, an open-source platform that helps governments simplify how people apply for benefits. CiviForm is stewarded by Exygy and was named one of TIME's Best Inventions of 2025. I'm also a product advisor for the nonprofit Illinois Digital Service (ILDS).

Before this, I spent five years at the Department of Veterans Affairs Office of the CTO. I led product on the flagship VA.gov benefits tools, including the effort to remediate the crisis of 100,000+ silently failed digital benefits submissions. Our work to make it right delivered $75M in backpay and waived $1.65M in unnecessary debt. My team also built first-of-its-kind automation software that saved Veterans 5,000+ waiting days by eliminating unnecessary exams when recent medical evidence already existed.

I got to VA through 18F, where I worked on federal technology projects across VA, DOD, and USDA. Prior to that, I was Head of Producers at Upstatement, where I guided strategic design and engineering projects for clients like On Being, PBS NewsHour, Harvard Gazette, Emergence Capital, and the MIT homepage and undergraduate admissions. I started my career as an editorial designer and illustrator at The Boston Globe and The Star Tribune in Minneapolis.
